## 1. Purpose

Not all information needs the same protection. Clear levels help people share freely where that is safe and protect what really matters.

## 2. Scope

It covers information in any form: documents, email, chats, databases, recordings and paper.

## 3. Classification levels

- Public: approved for anyone, such as published marketing material. No special handling.
- Internal: for staff and trusted partners, such as internal procedures. Do not publish; share externally only for a business need.
- Confidential: could harm {{COMPANY}}, customers or individuals if disclosed, such as contracts, financial results or personal data. Share only with named people who need it, and encrypt when sent outside the company.
- Restricted: the most sensitive information, such as board material, merger plans, security secrets or special categories of personal data. Access limited to a named group, encryption required and external sharing only with owner approval.

## 4. Labelling

- Apply the matching sensitivity label in Microsoft 365 when you create or receive information. When unsure, choose the higher level and ask the owner.
- Labels may apply encryption, watermarks and sharing limits automatically.
- Information without a label is treated as Internal.

## 5. Handling rules

- Store information only in approved company locations.
- Use sharing links scoped to specific people for Confidential and Restricted content; do not use 'anyone' links.
- Print Confidential or Restricted material only when needed, and collect it immediately.
- Dispose of information securely when no longer needed and in line with retention rules.

## 6. Roles

- Information owners decide the level, approve access and review it at least yearly.
- Everyone handles information according to its label and reports mislabelled or exposed information to {{SECURITY_CONTACT}}.

## 7. Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, explain the business reason and the risk, include compensating measures, and have an end date. {{OWNER}} approves or rejects them and keeps a record.

## 8. Compliance

Breaches of this policy may lead to access being removed and to action under employment or contract terms. Report suspected breaches to {{SECURITY_CONTACT}}.

## 9. Ownership and review

Policy owner: {{OWNER}}. Approved by: {{APPROVER}}. Effective from: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}. Next review: {{REVIEW_DATE}}.
