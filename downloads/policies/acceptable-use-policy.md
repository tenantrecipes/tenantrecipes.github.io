## 1. Purpose

This policy explains what good use of company IT looks like and which behaviour is not allowed. It protects {{COMPANY}}, its customers and you.

## 2. Scope

It covers every device, account, application and network that {{COMPANY}} owns, pays for or manages, wherever you use them, and any personal device used for work.

## 3. General principles

- Company IT is provided for work. Limited personal use is fine if it is lawful, does not affect your work and does not put company information at risk.
- You are responsible for everything done with your accounts. Never share passwords, MFA prompts or sign-in codes, and never approve an MFA request you did not start.
- Store work information only in company-approved locations, such as OneDrive, SharePoint or Teams, not on personal cloud storage or USB drives.
- Lock your screen when you leave your device, and keep devices with you or locked away when travelling.

## 4. Not allowed

- Accessing, storing or sending illegal, harassing, discriminatory or sexually explicit material.
- Installing software, browser extensions or apps that IT has not approved, or turning off security tools such as antivirus, firewall or disk encryption.
- Trying to get around security controls, test systems for weaknesses, or access information you are not authorised to see.
- Forwarding company email automatically to personal accounts, or copying customer or personal data to private devices or services.
- Using company IT for private business, cryptocurrency mining or anything that could damage {{COMPANY}}'s reputation.

## 5. Email, messaging and the internet

- Treat unexpected links, attachments and payment or bank-detail change requests with suspicion. Report suspected phishing with the report button or to {{IT_SERVICE_DESK}}.
- Write messages as if they could be read by a customer or a court; they are company records.
- Use only approved collaboration tools for company information; external sharing must follow the information classification policy.

## 6. Monitoring and privacy

{{COMPANY}} logs and monitors the use of its IT to protect its systems and meet legal duties. Monitoring is proportionate, follows applicable law and is described in the employee privacy notice. Personal content is only reviewed when there is a legitimate, documented reason.

## 7. Reporting

Report lost or stolen devices, suspected security incidents or breaches of this policy to {{IT_SERVICE_DESK}} or {{SECURITY_CONTACT}} straight away. Reporting early is always better than fixing it yourself, and honest reports are not punished.

## 8. Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, explain the business reason and the risk, include compensating measures, and have an end date. {{OWNER}} approves or rejects them and keeps a record.

## 9. Compliance

Breaches of this policy may lead to access being removed and to action under employment or contract terms. Report suspected breaches to {{SECURITY_CONTACT}}.

## 10. Ownership and review

Policy owner: {{OWNER}}. Approved by: {{APPROVER}}. Effective from: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}. Next review: {{REVIEW_DATE}}.
