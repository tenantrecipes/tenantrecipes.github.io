## 1. Purpose

Mobile and personal devices make flexible work possible, but they are easy to lose and harder to control. This policy balances your privacy with the need to protect company data.

## 2. Scope

It covers company-owned devices and personal devices (BYOD) used to access company email, Microsoft 365 apps, files or business systems.

## 3. Minimum requirements for any device

- A supported operating system that receives security updates, kept up to date.
- A screen lock with a PIN, password or biometrics, and automatic locking after a short idle time.
- Device encryption switched on.
- No jailbroken or rooted devices.
- Apps installed only from the official app stores.

## 4. Company-owned devices

- Company devices are enrolled in {{MDM_TOOL}} and fully managed by IT.
- IT can apply settings, install apps, locate the device and wipe it if it is lost, stolen or returned.

## 5. Personal devices (BYOD)

- Company data on personal devices stays inside approved apps protected by app protection policies (for example Outlook, Teams and OneDrive).
- Copying company data to personal apps or storage, or taking screenshots of restricted information, may be blocked.
- IT can remove company data and accounts from the device, but does not wipe your personal content and does not see your personal photos, messages or browsing.
- Access may be limited to web or approved apps if the device does not meet the requirements above.

## 6. Loss, theft and leaving

- Report a lost or stolen device to {{IT_SERVICE_DESK}} as soon as possible, and no later than the same working day.
- When you leave {{COMPANY}} or replace a device, company data is removed from it and company devices are returned.

## 7. Privacy

{{COMPANY}} only collects the device information needed to check compliance and protect company data. What is visible to IT is described in the employee privacy notice.

## 8. Exceptions

Exceptions must be requested in writing, explain the business reason and the risk, include compensating measures, and have an end date. {{OWNER}} approves or rejects them and keeps a record.

## 9. Compliance

Breaches of this policy may lead to access being removed and to action under employment or contract terms. Report suspected breaches to {{SECURITY_CONTACT}}.

## 10. Ownership and review

Policy owner: {{OWNER}}. Approved by: {{APPROVER}}. Effective from: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}. Next review: {{REVIEW_DATE}}.
